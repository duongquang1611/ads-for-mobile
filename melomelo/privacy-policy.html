Melomelo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Melomelo ("the app") is a casual fruit-merge puzzle game published by Melomelo. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we do NOT collect

The app has no account system and no server of its own. Your game progress (scores, coins, unlocks, settings) is stored only on your device. Photos you attach to fruits via the "Custom Faces" feature are cropped and stored only on your device — they are never uploaded anywhere.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and coarse ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob data usage. In the European Economic Area and the UK, a consent dialog is shown before any ad is loaded; you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy & ad consent.

Permissions

  • Camera / Photos — used only when you choose to put your own photo on a fruit. The image never leaves your device.
  • Network — used only to load ads.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad serving follows Google Play Families ad requirements where applicable.

Data deletion

All app data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app, or using Android's "Clear storage", removes it completely.
